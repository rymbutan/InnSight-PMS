These power the Presidential free-roam suite. They were generated by the project
owner with Meshy AI (image-to-3D) and renamed here to stable keys (originals were
`Meshy_AI_<name>_<id>_image-to-3d-texture.glb`). Treat per the owner's Meshy
licensing.

then a default size in `../RoomFreeRoam.jsx` MODEL_CFG.
